# Swipe to hire: pick your agents like a dating app

**Priority:** low — a nicer way in to something that already works (tap a vacancy, «Que empiece»)
**Gate:** none
**Next:** a first deck on Equipo, behind the existing hire step, then on a new organisation's first Hoy

Álvaro's idea (2026-11-20): at some point, picking the agents you work with feels like Tinder. He answered the four questions on 2026-11-21, taking every proposed answer, in the first decision deck (decision-deck.md).

## The idea

A deck of cards, one per agent you could have. Each card is the ghost (live 3D, its signature move), its job in one line, and what it would do first for *your* organisation, read from the record («Tengo 14 facturas sin cuadrar de octubre; empiezo por ahí»). Swipe right and it joins the team and arrives talking: the same hire step as today's `contratar` (team-chat.md, decision 8). Swipe left and it stays a vacancy. A tap on the card turns it over: what it reads, what it may do on its own, what it always asks you first.

## Why it fits

- **The way in asks too much cold.** «Toca para que empiece» on five ghosts is a lot to choose from (entry-flows.md, proposed change 4). One card at a time is one choice at a time.
- **It is a game** without a game layer: the ghosts were made to be looked at (gamification.md).

## Decisions (Álvaro, 2026-11-21, in the decision deck)

1. **Where the deck lives.** Decided: on the first Hoy of a new organisation, in place of the list of vacancies, and behind «Contratar» on Equipo. The tap on a vacancy stays, for anyone who doesn't swipe.
2. **What a card is.** One card per job, or several candidates for the same job (a careful Finanzas and a fast one, a cheaper model and a stronger one). Decided: one per job now; candidates once model routing (model-routing.md) makes them really differ.
3. **What a left swipe means.** Decided: the job stays a vacancy on Equipo, and the deck doesn't offer it again for a while; it is never «rejected» for good.
4. **Who may swipe.** Decided: owners only, as with hiring today; a member's right swipe could become a request to an owner later.
