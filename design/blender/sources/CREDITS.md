# Borrowed models

Free models `ghost.py` starts some props from. It keeps only their shape (rescaled,
turned, partly dropped); colours come from the app's shaders. Both are CC-BY 3.0, so
**the app must credit them** wherever it lists credits once it ships.

| File | Model | Author | Licence | Source | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `office-phone.glb` | Office Phone | dook | CC-BY 3.0 | https://poly.pizza/m/YxfMuchpUF | The Secretaría's handset (its `Handle` only) |
| `green-notebook.glb` | Green Notebook | Ryan Donaldson | CC-BY 3.0 | https://poly.pizza/m/a-pdTVJ1F46 | The Secretaría's spiral notepad (pages, covers, wire) |

Searched 2026-11-14 on Poly Pizza and Poly Haven. Nothing free fitted the hair bun or
the scarf (free hair models are realistic hair cards), so those stay modelled in
`ghost.py`; the next step for them is Meshy, then an artist.
